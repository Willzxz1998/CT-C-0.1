# Circular Cultivation and Chemistry SusTool

## Purpose
This research tool supports circular valorization of horticultural residues in the SNF region by providing interactive production, residue, and conversion-potential analytics.

## Functions
- Visualize horticultural production and residue inventory (2022, provincial level).
- Evaluate potential biochar and compost production under user-defined residue utilization scenarios.
- Compare crops, provinces, and residue types through ranking, contribution, overview, and map views.

## Manual
1. Open **Tool data** from the left sidebar.
2. Select data type, year, provinces, crops, and residue type.
3. For potential products, set residue utilization ratio.
4. Use **Reload data** after updating `data/CTCdata.xlsx`.

## Scope
- Geography: Southern Netherlands and Flanders (SNF), NUTS2.
- Provinces: Zeeland, Noord Brabant, Limburg (NL), East Flanders, West Flanders, Flemish Brabant, Antwerp Province, Limburg (BE).
- Data source: `data/CTCdata.xlsx`.
